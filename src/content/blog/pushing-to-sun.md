I never thought the semantics of a name could bug me so much but it does.

I pushed to master because that’s what I learned starting out, and I continued pushing to master because it was cool to have my own master branch.

But now the master branch is tainted, regardless of what I think or feel. Pushing to main just feels neutered in comparison. And pushing to trunk sounds silly because trunks are not branches, trunks are that which support branches, and I don’t want to push to trunk just to support both master and main branches.

Beyond branches is sky, and beyond sky is space, and in space is sun. The sun is what sustains us, the sun is our source, there is no future without sun.

So now I push to sun, because it's cooler than master and 1 character shorter than main.
